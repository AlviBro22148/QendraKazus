# Surface brief: Qendra KAZUS website (all routes)

Scope: Persuade (home, projects), Read (posts, about, notifications). Rebuild of qendrakazus.com as a static React site.
Audience/job: Albanian youth and partners; understand the NGO, read projects and news, act (contact, sign petition, answer calls).
Constraints: palette pinned (white, sky blue), light only, all content preserved, bilingual.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard played straight: an NGO site where the organisation's own photographs and words carry the page; it refuses the stock-template tells (icon-card trios, kickers, gradient hero).
OWN-WORLD: White ground, #E0F2FE tint bands, #0EA5E9 / #0284C7 for actions and one drenched sky-blue band; ink #0F172A. Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, Figtree for text, self-hosted. Photos shown large, uncropped-feeling, with slight rounded corners (8px) and no ornament.
STORY: Visitor learns the mission within one screen, sees real youth activity, reads latest posts, finds projects, and can write or call.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the mission statement as a large Bricolage headline with one primary button (MË SHUMË to contact); right, three real photos staggered at different sizes on a pale-blue field; header with logo, nav and language button above.
